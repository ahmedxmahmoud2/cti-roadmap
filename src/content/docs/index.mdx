This roadmap takes you from IOC collector to intelligence analyst: someone who can investigate, connect evidence, analyse it, and anticipate an adversary's next move. It is one path. Finish a step, then move to the next one.

<EntryWizard />

## The path

0. [Technical foundations](/cti-roadmap/path/foundations/): networking, security basics, operating systems and scripting.
1. [CTI fundamentals and ATT&CK](/cti-roadmap/path/cti-fundamentals/): the intelligence lifecycle, requirements, confidence and ATT&CK.
2. [Reading and writing intelligence](/cti-roadmap/path/intel-writing/): reading reports critically and writing for decisions.
3. [OSINT and infrastructure pivoting](/cti-roadmap/path/osint-infrastructure/): domains, IPs, certificates and pivoting.
4. [Dark web and the underground](/cti-roadmap/path/dark-web/): credentials, brand abuse, ransomware and cybercrime markets.
5. [Threat actor profiling and tracking](/cti-roadmap/path/actor-profiling/): actor profiles, tracking over time and attribution.
6. [Malware basics for CTI](/cti-roadmap/path/malware/): enough to triage a sample and pull intelligence out of it.
7. [Hunting and detection basics for CTI](/cti-roadmap/path/hunting-detection/): enough to work with the teams who turn your intelligence into hunts and rules.
8. [Platforms and automation](/cti-roadmap/path/platforms-automation/): STIX, MISP, OpenCTI and your own pipeline.
9. [Advanced research and specialization](/cti-roadmap/path/specialization/): pick the area you go deep in.
10. [Portfolio and readiness](/cti-roadmap/path/portfolio/): what you should have built and be able to do.

## How each step works

Every step has one main course to study from, an Arabic option where a good one exists, and a short optional list if you want more. Then what you will learn, a practical project, and what you should be able to do before moving on. The **Next** link at the bottom of each page takes you to the following step.

## Time

At 8–10 hours a week, the full path takes roughly 9–12 months. Some steps overlap, and reaching expert level takes continuous practice after that.

A suggested week:

- 3 hours studying the course for your current step.
- 2 hours reading real threat reports.
- 3 hours of hands-on labs.
- 2 hours of research and writing.

## Ground rules

- **Free first.** Every step has a free option. Paid courses are marked as paid.
- **Fact vs assessment.** In everything you write, separate what you observed from what you assess, and say how confident you are.
- **Stay legal and safe.** Read [Ethics, OPSEC and law](/cti-roadmap/ethics/) before you touch malware or underground sources.
