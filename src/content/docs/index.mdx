## Why this roadmap

Most CTI roadmaps are reading lists. This one is built around what employers actually hire for: turning a question into evidence, evidence into a judgment, and a judgment into something a decision-maker can act on.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Start where you are" icon="rocket">
		Five entry points (zero, SOC, pentest, developer, non-technical) and a self-check at every level, so you skip what you already know.
	</Card>
	<Card title="A deliverable every level" icon="document">
		Every level ends in a written product for your portfolio. Writing appears in almost every CTI job posting; it is not optional here.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Pick your tracks" icon="random">
		After the shared core, specialise in one or two tracks: infrastructure, malware, hunting, dark web, strategic, engineering or intel-led red teaming.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Regional focus" icon="translate">
		Case studies and sources on actors targeting the Middle East and North Africa, plus Arabic resources where they exist.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
