This roadmap takes you from IOC collector to intelligence analyst: someone who can investigate, connect evidence, analyse it, and anticipate an adversary's next move. It is one path. Finish a step, then move to the next one.

<EntryWizard />

## The path

0. [Technical foundations](/cti-roadmap/path/00-foundations/): networking, operating systems and scripting.
1. [CTI fundamentals and the intelligence lifecycle](/cti-roadmap/path/01-cti-fundamentals/): requirements, analysis and confidence.
2. [OSINT and intelligence collection](/cti-roadmap/path/02-osint-collection/): technical OSINT and infrastructure pivoting.
3. [MITRE ATT&CK and adversary analysis](/cti-roadmap/path/03-attack-adversary-analysis/): TTPs and campaign analysis.
4. [Malware intelligence](/cti-roadmap/path/04-malware-intelligence/): static and dynamic analysis, and pulling intelligence out of samples.
5. [Threat hunting and detection engineering](/cti-roadmap/path/05-hunting-detection/): turning intelligence into hunts and rules.
6. [Threat actor profiling, tracking and attribution](/cti-roadmap/path/06-actor-tracking-attribution/): building actor profiles and following them over time.
7. [Dark web and the underground ecosystem](/cti-roadmap/path/07-dark-web/): cybercrime markets, credentials, ransomware and brand abuse.
8. [CTI engineering and intelligence platforms](/cti-roadmap/path/08-cti-engineering/): STIX, MISP, OpenCTI and your own pipeline.
9. [Advanced research and specialization](/cti-roadmap/path/09-specialization/): pick the area you go deep in.
10. [Portfolio and readiness](/cti-roadmap/path/10-portfolio/): what you should have built and be able to do.

## How each step works

Every step tells you what you will learn, which course to study from, which tools to use, and a practical project. A step ends with a short list of what you should be able to do before moving on. The **Next** link at the bottom of each page takes you to the following step.

## Time

At 8–10 hours a week, the full path takes roughly 9–12 months. Some steps overlap, and reaching expert level takes continuous practice after that.

A suggested week:

- 3 hours studying the course for your current step.
- 2 hours reading real threat reports.
- 3 hours of hands-on labs.
- 2 hours of research and writing.

## Ground rules

- **Free first.** Every step has a free option. Paid courses are marked as paid.
- **Fact vs assessment.** In everything you write, separate what you observed from what you assess, and say how confident you are.
- **Stay legal and safe.** Read [Ethics, OPSEC and law](/cti-roadmap/ethics/) before you touch malware or underground sources.
