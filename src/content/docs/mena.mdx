Most public CTI training uses examples from the US and Europe. This page collects sources for the threats that actually target organisations in the Middle East and North Africa, so you can practise on them.

## Case studies to practise on

- **MuddyWater (ESET, December 2025).** [MuddyWater: Snakes by the riverbank](https://www.welivesecurity.com/en/eset-research/muddywater-snakes-riverbank/) covers a campaign mainly against Israeli organisations with a confirmed Egyptian victim, new malware, and a joint operation with an OilRig subgroup. A good case study for the [actor tracking project](/cti-roadmap/path/actor-profiling/).
- **The original MuddyWater naming.** Palo Alto Unit 42’s 2017 blog *Muddying the Water: Targeted Attacks in the Middle East* is where the name comes from. Read it next to recent reporting and compare how the actor and its tooling have changed.

## Landscape reports

- **CloudSEK, [Middle East Cyber Threat Landscape 2025–2026](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635e632477408d12d1811a64/6aa9246a81909ec612530547_Upload_CloudSEK_Middle_East_Cyber_Threat_Landscape_2025-2026.pdf).** Ransomware and hacktivism trends by country.
- **Naif Arab University for Security Sciences, *Ransomware Trends Report in Arab Countries 2025*.** Victims, groups and countries, built from leak-site data and incident reporting.
- **Microsoft Digital Defense Report.** Global report with a regional breakdown for the Middle East.

<Aside type="caution" title="Read the numbers critically">
Vendor “indicator” or “feed” counts are not confirmed incidents, and leak-site claims are not verified breaches. Rewriting a landscape report’s numbers with their limits stated is a good source-evaluation exercise.
</Aside>

## Vendor research with strong regional coverage

- ESET Research, Check Point Research, Kaspersky GReAT, Palo Alto Unit 42, Group-IB, Microsoft Threat Intelligence, Google Threat Intelligence (Mandiant).
- Citizen Lab and Amnesty Tech for spyware targeting journalists and civil society in the region; a good starting point for analysts from journalism or politics.

## National CERTs and authorities

- **Egypt:** [EG-CERT](https://egcert.eg/about-us/) (under NTRA), with public alerts and incident reporting.
- **Saudi Arabia:** the [National Cybersecurity Authority](https://nca.gov.sa/en/services/information-enrichment/) runs an information-enrichment service for sharing indicators.
- **Region:** OIC-CERT links national CERTs across member states, including CERT-SA, Q-CERT, OCERT and JO-CERT.

## Actor names you will meet

Vendors name the same actor differently. Learn to map them, and never assume two names mean the same group without checking.

- **MuddyWater** — also tracked as Mango Sandstorm and Seedworm.
- **OilRig / APT34** — and related subgroups such as Lyceum.
- Iranian, other state-linked and financially motivated actors also target the region; ransomware groups and hacktivist collectives dominate by volume.
