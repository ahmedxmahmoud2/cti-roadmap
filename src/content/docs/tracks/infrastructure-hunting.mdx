Adversaries reuse hosting providers, certificate patterns, server configurations and registration habits. This track teaches you to turn those habits into queries that find new infrastructure on your own, without waiting for someone else's IOCs.

**Fits:** pentesters, network engineers, SOC analysts who liked pivoting in Level 2.

**Prerequisite:** Levels 1 and 2. **Time:** 3–6 months.
