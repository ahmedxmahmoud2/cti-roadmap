After Level 2 you have the shared core. Now go deep in **one or two** tracks. Trying all seven at once is how people end up knowing a little about everything. Pin your tracks on [My progress](/cti-roadmap/progress/) to follow them.

**Time:** 3–6 months per track to reach a solid level.

## Good combinations

- **Infrastructure hunting + Dark web and digital risk:** the profile most MSSPs and DRP vendors look for.
- **Threat hunting and detection + Malware intelligence:** the most technical defensive profile.
- **Strategic and geopolitical + any technical track:** rare and valuable; most analysts are one or the other.
- **CTI engineering + anything:** teams always need someone who can automate.
