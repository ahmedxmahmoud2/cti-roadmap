Strategic intelligence answers questions leaders ask: what is changing, what it means for us, and what we should do about it. It is where analytic tradecraft and writing matter most.

**Fits:** people from politics, international relations, journalism or risk; and technical analysts who want to move toward leadership.

**Prerequisite:** Levels 1 and 2. **Time:** 3–6 months.
