Most companies meet CTI through this track: leaked credentials, ransomware claims, phishing sites using their brand, fraud. The skill is not finding data, it is validating it and turning it into an action the client can take.

**Fits:** DRP and MSSP analysts, fraud teams. Fraud and third-party risk are part of this track because they share sources and methods.

**Prerequisite:** Levels 1 and 2. **Time:** 3–6 months.
