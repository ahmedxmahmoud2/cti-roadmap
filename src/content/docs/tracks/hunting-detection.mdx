Intelligence that never reaches a detection or a decision is a report nobody used. This track connects CTI to the SOC: hypotheses, queries, rules, and the evidence that something is or is not happening in your environment.

**Fits:** SOC and IR analysts. Vulnerability intelligence (CVSS, EPSS, KEV) is included here as a module.

**Prerequisite:** Levels 1 and 2. **Time:** 3–6 months.
