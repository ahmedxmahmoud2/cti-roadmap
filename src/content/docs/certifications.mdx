Certifications are proof, not learning. None of them is an exit criterion in this roadmap: a strong portfolio does more for you than a certificate. If an employer pays, they can help with HR filters.

<Aside type="caution" title="Prices change">
Prices below were published in late 2026 and vary by country and package. Check the provider before you book.
</Aside>

## Entry level

- **arcX Foundation (free).** Comes with the free CTI 101 course. Arabic subtitles available.
- **CREST CPTIA (Practitioner Threat Intelligence Analyst).** Around £275. No experience required; multiple-choice exam.

## Experienced analysts

- **Mandiant MCTIA.** Around $250 per attempt; 3–5 years’ experience recommended.
- **CREST CRTIA (Registered).** Around £600. Multiple choice plus long written answers; about two years’ experience expected. Its syllabus is a useful checklist even if you never sit the exam.
- **EC-Council CTIA.** Roughly $1,000–$4,400 depending on the package.
- **GIAC GCTI.** About $999 for the exam alone. Pairs with SANS FOR578.

## Employer-funded

- **SANS FOR578: Cyber Threat Intelligence.** About $8,780 in the US. Excellent, but only realistic if your employer pays.
- **SANS FOR589: Cybercrime Intelligence.** Underground markets and crypto tracing; for the dark web track.
- **CREST CCTIM.** For people leading CTI teams.
