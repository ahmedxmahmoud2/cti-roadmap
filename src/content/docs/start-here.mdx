The roadmap has a shared core (Levels 0–2) and specialisation tracks (Level 3). Where you start depends on where you come from.

<EntryWizard />

## How the roadmap works

<Steps>

1. **Run the self-check** at the top of your starting level. If you can tick every box honestly, jump to the next level.

2. **Work through the topics.** Tick them off as you go. Progress is saved in your browser; export it from [My progress](/cti-roadmap/progress/) to move it to another device.

3. **Produce the deliverables.** A level is not done when you finish reading; it is done when the deliverable exists. Publish what you can (blog, GitHub, LinkedIn) without client or sensitive data.

4. **Meet the exit criteria**, then move on. After Level 2, pick one or two [tracks](/cti-roadmap/tracks/).

</Steps>

## Time

Estimates assume **8–10 hours a week**. They are ranges, not a race.

- **Level 0 · Foundations:** 3–6 months from zero, a few weeks if you are only filling gaps.
- **Level 1 · CTI core:** 2–3 months.
- **Level 2 · Core skills:** 3–4 months.
- **Level 3 · Tracks:** 3–6 months per track, and then it never really ends.

A suggested week: 3 hours learning, 2 hours reading real threat reports, 3 hours hands-on, 2 hours writing.

## Ground rules

- **Free first.** Every level can be completed with free resources. Paid courses and [certifications](/cti-roadmap/certifications/) are optional.
- **Fact vs judgment.** In every product you write, separate what you observed from what you assess, and say how confident you are.
- **Stay legal and safe.** Read [Ethics, OPSEC and law](/cti-roadmap/ethics/) before you touch underground sources or malware.
