This roadmap is open source and improves when practitioners contribute.

## Suggest a resource

Open a [resource suggestion](https://github.com/ahmedxmahmoud2/cti-roadmap/issues/new?template=resource.yml) with the link, what it is, what it costs and where it fits. Free and Arabic resources are especially welcome.

## Edit content

Every page has an **Edit page** link at the bottom. Small fixes can go straight in as a pull request.

Where things live:

- `src/content/docs/path/` — the steps of the path, one page per step.
- `src/data/path.ts` — the list of steps and the starting point for each background.
- `src/content/docs/` — the other English pages.
- `src/content/docs/ar/` — Arabic pages. A missing Arabic page falls back to English automatically.

## Translate

Arabic translations are welcome page by page. Keep English technical terms as they are and follow the [glossary](/cti-roadmap/glossary/) for the Arabic ones.

## Rules for content

- Free first. Paid resources are welcome but must be marked as paid.
- No links to stolen data, leak downloads, or anything illegal to access.
- Vendor content is fine when it teaches; marketing pages are not.
