## What you will learn

### Understanding CTI

The difference between cyber threat intelligence, threat hunting, incident response, digital forensics, OSINT, security research and threat monitoring.

The four levels of intelligence:

- **Strategic:** supports management decisions. Example: is the financial sector in our region increasingly targeted by ransomware groups?
- **Operational:** campaigns, attacks and threat actors. Example: an APT campaign targeting banks with phishing and custom malware.
- **Tactical:** the TTPs used in an attack. Example: a group using DLL side-loading and scheduled tasks for persistence.
- **Technical:** directly usable data. Example: IPs, domains, hashes, URLs, YARA rules.

### The intelligence lifecycle

Planning and direction, collection, processing and exploitation, analysis and production, dissemination, feedback. In practice it is not linear: analysis often shows you need more collection.

### Intelligence requirements

Learn to write Priority Intelligence Requirements (PIRs), Specific Intelligence Requirements (SIRs), collection requirements, intelligence gaps and essential elements of information.

Example PIR: *What APT groups are targeting financial organizations in the Middle East?* Break it into smaller questions:

- Which groups have targeted this sector?
- Which malware families are associated with them?
- What infrastructure do they use?
- Which initial access techniques have been observed?
- What are their latest campaigns?

### Intelligence analysis

Source reliability, information credibility, confidence levels, intelligence gaps, evidence correlation, alternative hypotheses, analytical bias and structured analytic techniques. Focus on:

- Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH).
- Link analysis.
- Timeline analysis.
- Hypothesis testing.
- Confidence assessment.

## Project: APT intelligence profile

Pick one APT group and write an intelligence profile with: overview, known aliases, targeted industries and countries, motivation, known campaigns, malware families, infrastructure, TTPs, sources and a confidence assessment.

Every statement needs a source, and you must separate facts from assessments. Use the [Threat Actor Profile Guide](https://www.curatedintel.org/2023/07/the-threat-actor-profile-guide-for-cti.html) from Curated Intelligence as your template. You will go much deeper on profiling in step 6.

## You are ready to move on when

- You can turn a business question into PIRs and smaller collectable questions.
- You can grade a source and a piece of information, and state a judgment with a confidence level.
- Your APT profile cites a source for every claim.
