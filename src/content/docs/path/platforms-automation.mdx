## What you will learn

### Intelligence data modeling

STIX 2.1, TAXII 2.1, the MISP data model, STIX objects and relationships, observables, indicators, malware, threat actor, campaign and report objects, confidence and validity.

### MISP

Events, attributes, objects, galaxies, taxonomies, warning lists, correlation, feeds, sharing groups, API integration, PyMISP.

### OpenCTI

The knowledge graph, threat actor modeling, campaign relationships, intrusion sets, observables, indicators, reports, external references, connectors, ingestion pipelines, import and export, graph analysis, the API and STIX 2.1 integration.

### CTI automation

With Python, build: IOC collection and enrichment pipelines, threat feed integration, deduplication, normalisation, expiration management, confidence scoring, automated correlation, report generation, and OpenCTI and MISP integration.

### CTI data quality

Duplicate indicators, false positives, stale IOCs, expired infrastructure, incorrect attribution, source reliability, data provenance, confidence, indicator revocation and relationship validation. Never treat an IOC as malicious just because it appears in a feed.

## Project: build your own CTI pipeline

1. **External sources:** OSINT, threat feeds, reports, APIs.
2. **Python collection and processing:** parsing, normalisation, deduplication.
3. **Enrichment and correlation:** reputation, relationships, context.
4. **MISP** for IOC management.
5. **OpenCTI** as the knowledge graph.
6. **Operational output:** SIEM, XDR, threat hunting, client reports.

Done well, this is one of the strongest projects in your portfolio.

## You are ready to move on when

- You can model an actor, a campaign and their indicators in STIX.
- Your pipeline ingests, deduplicates, enriches and expires data on its own.
