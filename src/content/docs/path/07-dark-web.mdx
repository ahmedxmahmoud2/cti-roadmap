## What you will learn

### The underground ecosystem

Cybercrime forums, initial access brokers, ransomware groups, leak sites, stealer log markets, credential markets, botnet services, malware-as-a-service, ransomware-as-a-service, exploit trading and data brokerage.

### Dark web collection

Source discovery, forum and marketplace monitoring, Telegram monitoring, paste sites, data leak monitoring, automated collection, source reliability and operational security.

### Credential intelligence

Infostealer logs, session cookies, browser credentials, credential reuse, corporate email exposure, initial access listings, authentication context and exposure validation.

### Brand protection intelligence

Brand impersonation, typosquatting, phishing infrastructure, fake social media accounts, fake applications, fraudulent domains, lookalike domains and brand abuse campaigns.

### Ransomware intelligence

Ransomware group profiles, leak site monitoring, victimology, affiliates, double extortion, data exfiltration, initial access brokers and ransomware infrastructure.

## Project: brand exposure investigation

Investigate a fictional brand and report on: exposed assets, impersonating domains, leaked credentials, related threat actors, source evidence, a risk assessment and recommended actions.

Data published on an underground source does not prove it is recent or accurate, or that the organisation was recently breached.

## You are ready to move on when

- You can explain how stolen data moves through the underground economy.
- You can validate an exposure claim before you report it.
