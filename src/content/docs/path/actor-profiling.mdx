## On the job

You keep profiles for the actors that matter to your organisation or your clients' sector, and update them when new reporting appears. When management asks "who is this group and should we worry?", the profile is your answer.

## What you will learn

### Threat actor profiling

For every actor you follow, build a profile with: known aliases, suspected origin or sponsorship, motivation, targeted sectors and geography, known campaigns, malware families, infrastructure, TTPs, operational patterns, historical activity and latest developments.

### APT tracking

Continuous monitoring, not just following the news. Track new campaigns, new infrastructure, new malware, TTP changes, victimology changes, exploited vulnerabilities, new initial access methods, changes in C2 infrastructure, and relationships with other groups.

### Attribution analysis

One of the hardest parts of CTI. Study technical, infrastructure and behavioural attribution, malware code similarities, linguistic analysis, operational patterns, false flag operations, attribution confidence and competing hypotheses.

Know the difference between confirmed attribution and high, moderate and low confidence assessments. Never attribute a campaign to a group just because the report you are reading did. Review the evidence and understand what the attribution rests on.

### Campaign clustering

Link attacks using shared infrastructure, shared malware, similar C2 configuration, similar TTPs, victim overlap, temporal correlation and shared operational artifacts.

## Project: 30-day actor tracking

Pick one APT group, for example one targeting the Middle East (see [MENA threat landscape](/cti-roadmap/mena/)). Track it for 30 days:

- Record every new campaign and new infrastructure.
- Review its malware families.
- Compare old and new TTPs.
- Record any change in targeting.
- Write a weekly intelligence update.

At the end, write a tracking report: what changed, what stayed the same, and what is evidence versus assessment.

## You are ready to move on when

- You can build a full actor profile with sources and confidence.
- You can explain what an attribution claim is based on, and how confident it deserves to be.
