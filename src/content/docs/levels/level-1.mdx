**Who this is for:** everyone. **Time:** 2–3 months.

This level is what separates an intelligence analyst from an IOC collector. It is mostly about thinking and writing, not tools. Teams struggle here in practice: the SANS 2026 CTI survey found most CISOs value CTI, yet only about a quarter say it significantly shapes their decisions, and around half of programs collect no systematic feedback. Requirements, stakeholders and clear writing close that gap.

## 3. Requirements, stakeholders and feedback

Everything starts with a question someone needs answered. Learn to find that question, scope it, answer it, and check whether the answer helped.

<Checklist list="l1.req" title="Requirements" />

<Aside type="tip" title="Example">
**Business question:** “Should we worry about ransomware hitting our payment systems in Egypt this year?”
**PIR:** Which ransomware groups target financial services in Egypt and the wider MENA region, and how do they get in?
**SIRs:** Which groups posted MENA financial victims in the last 12 months? Which initial access methods did they use? Which of those methods apply to our exposed services?
</Aside>
