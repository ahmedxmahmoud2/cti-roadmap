**Who this is for:** people starting from zero, and anyone who fails the self-check below. **Time:** 3–6 months from zero at 8–10 hours a week.

You cannot analyse an attack you do not understand. An analyst who cannot read a PCAP, a process tree or a DNS record ends up copying other people’s IOCs. This level gives you enough networking, operating systems, scripting and security basics to understand what you read in a threat report.

This roadmap does not re-teach these basics; it points you to good free courses (several in Arabic) and tells you when you are done.
